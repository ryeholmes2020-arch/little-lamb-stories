Privacy Policy

Little Lamb Stories is an ad-free companion website for our family storytelling videos.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

What we collect

This website does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit forms, provide names or ages, or sign up for email. We do not run advertising, analytics, remarketing pixels, or behavioral tracking on this site.

Video and third-party services

Our pages link to and embed videos from our official Little Lamb Stories YouTube channel. YouTube may process information according to its own privacy policy when a visitor interacts with the YouTube player or leaves this site for YouTube.

We use Google Fonts to display the site typography. The site is hosted and delivered by Vercel. These providers may receive ordinary technical request information needed to deliver their services.

Children and families

This is a family-oriented website. We intentionally avoid collecting personal information from children and do not provide chat, comments, accounts, or personalized advertising on the site. Parents should supervise children’s use of external services, including YouTube, and review the audience settings on each YouTube video.

Cookies and tracking

Little Lamb Stories does not intentionally set cookies or use local storage for tracking. Third-party services, including YouTube, may use their own technologies when loaded or visited. Privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds are used where available, but they do not replace YouTube’s own privacy notice.

External links

Links to YouTube and other external services are provided so families can watch official content. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Contact

For questions about this website or this policy, contact us through the official Little Lamb Stories YouTube channel.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the site’s features or services change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
